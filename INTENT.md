# augur

augur looks at a change and says how much care it deserves, as one of three words a person or a script can act on straight away. It exists because writing code has become cheap and review attention has not: humans need to know which tenth of a big diff to actually read, and agents need a signal that tells them to stop and escalate rather than merge blind. The judgement has to be earned from the repository itself rather than from a model or a service, it has to come out the same every time, and it has to say out loud what it is not sure about. It should feel like a senior engineer glancing at a pull request and pointing at the two files that matter — quick, specific, and never mysterious.
